> 16 waking hours ÷ 10 minutes = 96
>
> 10 minutes is ~1% of your day.

\- [credit to Taylor Troesh](https://taylor.town/10-minutes)

When the phone scrolling lasts for 10 minutes, or low-priority yak shaving adds up to 10 minutes, that's one percent of your day spent.
That's not necessarily a bad thing or a good thing, but make sure it's a thing you want to have spent one percent of your day on.
